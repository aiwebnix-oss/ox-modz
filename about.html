How verification works

Before any app appears on OX MODZ, it passes through a multi-layered security pipeline. We combine automated scanning with manual review to ensure integrity at every step.

01 · source_validation

We verify the origin of every APK, checking developer signatures against known trusted sources.

02 · malware_scan

Multiple antivirus engines scan each file. Any detection — even heuristic — triggers a full rejection.

03 · device_test

Apps are installed and run on physical Android devices to confirm functionality and detect anomalies.

04 · monitor

Even after publication, we re-scan apps periodically and monitor for emerging threats.
